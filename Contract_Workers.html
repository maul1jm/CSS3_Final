In the 1970s East Germany signed a compact with Vietnam to begin a contract worker program (Vertragsarbeiter). The impetus for the program was to bolster the East German economy with workers who would stay for a pre-determined amount of time. Contract workers interacted little with the East German population and lived in company-owned housing outside of urban areas. Rules governing the program and its participants were stringent and workers could be sent home for small violations. The pictures below illustrate typical jobs and housing that workers would have and portray workers as happy additions to East German society, which was not always the case. After the fall of the Berlin Wall, many workers found themselves torn between staying in East Germany, where they made their livelihoods and their home country, Vietnam.
