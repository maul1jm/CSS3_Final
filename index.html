Learning about the DDR with Material Culture

This site is an example of how to use material culture in German language classrooms. Objects are curated for use in teaching about the Deutsche Demokratische Republik (DDR) aka German Democratic Republic (GDR). The pages for contract workers and daily life are examples of units that students may encounter when working with this content.
